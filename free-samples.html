How you can use these

Short version: print them as much as you like for the people you look after. Just don't sell them.

  • Print them at home as many times as you like — for yourself, your family, or someone you care for.
  • Care homes, day programs, classrooms and activity groups are welcome to print and use them.
  • Please don't resell them, or pass them off as your own work.
  • Please don't upload them to stock sites, print-on-demand shops, or free-file collections.

The book these come from

The Introvert's Cozy Survival Kit is an 8.5 × 11 in paperback with 70 coloring pages, printed single-sided so color never shows through to the next page, and laid out with wide margins so nothing gets lost in the spine.
