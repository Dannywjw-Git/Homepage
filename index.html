About the author

Danny Wang writes fiction and essays from a narrow room overlooking a city that never fully turns off the lights.

Before writing books, they spent a decade building software and staring at servers. That background taught them that every system has a quiet failure mode — and that the best stories are the ones that happen when the lights stay on but everyone else has gone home.

Their work has been shortlisted for small awards you've probably never heard of, which is exactly how they like it. They write slowly, read everything twice, and answer email on Sunday evenings.
