About

Who is behind the books.

Junwei Wang is an independent publisher based in Shanghai, China, working on line-art and print-ready book design. The studio focuses on the bold & easy style of coloring book — thick, confident lines and uncluttered pages — because it is the format that works for the widest range of people, including those who find dense detail tiring.

Books are produced end to end by one person: concept, design, layout, cover and publishing. Titles are published under the name Danny Wang. The studio was established in 2026, and this page is its official online home.
