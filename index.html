About

Who is behind the books.

Wren Studio is a one-person publishing studio based in Shanghai, China, working on line-art and print-ready book design. The studio focuses on the bold & easy style of coloring book — thick, confident lines and uncluttered pages — because it is the format that works for the widest range of people, including those who find dense detail tiring.

Books are produced end to end by one person: concept, character design, layout, cover and publishing. The first title, The Introvert's Cozy Survival Kit, was started in 2026 as a love letter to anyone who has ever paused at the door, and this page is its official online home.
